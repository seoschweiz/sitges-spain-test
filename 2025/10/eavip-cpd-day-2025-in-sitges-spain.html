EAVIP CPD Day 2025 in Sitges Spain

 

Advancing Veterinary Imaging Across Europe

This September, the coastal town of Sitges, near Barcelona, becomes a hub for veterinary imaging professionals as it hosts the EAVIP CPD Day 2025 — a continuing professional development event that brings together Europe’s leading experts in diagnostic imaging. Held on Saturday, 20th September 2025, this one-day congress offers a blend of high-level academic insight, practical workshops, and cross-disciplinary networking set against the Mediterranean charm of Sitges.

A Day Dedicated to Veterinary Imaging Innovation

The event begins with a keynote lecture by Gabriel Manso-Díaz and Henry Trem, followed by a series of specialized sessions covering ultrasound, MRI, CT, radiography, oncology, and communication in veterinary practice. The program runs from 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., featuring short, dynamic presentations designed to bridge research and real-world application.

Among the highlights:

  • “Small Animal Ultrasound – Why You Can Do This Too!” with Angie Lloyd-Jones and Julie Burnage, inspiring veterinary professionals to embrace ultrasound diagnostics with confidence.

  • Sarah Andrews from Amsterdam presents “MRI in Dogs and Cats: Tips and Tricks on How to Obtain the Best Images,” guiding participants through practical ways to enhance image quality.

  • Christin Rohde, a leading expert from Qalibra CT, shares insights on “Revolutionising Equine Medicine: The Power of CT Imaging.”

  • Catherine D’Helft and Antonella Puggioni explore “The Importance of Good Communication between Vets and Imaging Practitioners,” emphasizing collaboration in clinical workflows.

  • Beatriz Moreno Aguado and Prof. Dr. Tiziana Liuti contribute detailed lectures on small-animal radiography and orthopedic imaging, providing a comprehensive overview of current best practices.

Featured Experts in Veterinary Imaging

The speaker lineup reads like a “who’s who” of European veterinary imaging:

  • Angie Lloyd-Jones and Julie Burnage from Aspire UCS Ltd are pioneers in bringing human ultrasound standards into veterinary practice.

  • Dr. Catherine D’Helft, founding member of EAVIP, merges academic research with practical imaging protocol optimization.

  • Christin Rohde, with 18 years of experience in equine diagnostic imaging, discusses advanced CT and MRI applications.

  • Prof. Dr. Tiziana Liuti from the University of Edinburgh provides insights from her groundbreaking research on equine CT and radiography.

  • Antonella Puggioni, current EAVDI President, introduces new educational approaches using mixed reality in veterinary training.

  • Masha de Koning-Hoogeboom and Beatriz Moreno bring unique perspectives from Utrecht and Southern Counties Veterinary Specialists, respectively, emphasizing cross-species imaging innovation.

Sitges: Where Science Meets the Sea

Beyond its professional value, the event’s location adds an unmistakable Mediterranean touch. Sitges, just 30 minutes south of Barcelona, offers attendees the perfect blend of inspiration and relaxation. With its sun-washed promenades, vibrant cultural life, and coastal charm, it’s the ideal setting for professionals to network, reflect, and recharge.

Learning, Sharing, and Shaping the Future

The EAVIP CPD Day stands as a milestone for veterinary imaging in Europe, reinforcing the importance of lifelong learning, collaboration, and technological innovation. From ultrasound to radiotherapy, the program encourages participants to explore new tools, question conventions, and shape the next generation of veterinary care.
