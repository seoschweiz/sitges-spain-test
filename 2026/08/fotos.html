Scenes from Sitges

This evolving photo gallery collects visual impressions of Sitges: the Mediterranean waterfront, the compact historic centre, local architecture, cultural life and the changing atmosphere of the town through the seasons. Rather than treating Sitges as a single postcard view, the gallery is intended to show the details that give the destination its character.

A town best explored on foot

Many of the most memorable views appear within a short walk of one another. The seafront leads naturally toward the old town, while narrow streets reveal galleries, façades, cafés and unexpected glimpses of the sea. More photographs will be added as the collection grows.
